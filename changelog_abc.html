Official release notes

Zombie ABC Changelog

Features, improvements and fixes for Zombie ABC: 3D Online Shooter from Snow Bat. Download the PC version (Windows).

Version 2.0.5

Fixed

  • Fixed a bug that caused zombies to freeze.
  • Fixed a bug affecting the evacuation message.

What's new

  • Added new achievements.

Version 2.0.4

Fixed

  • Fixed bullets getting stuck in the evacuation-zone trigger.
  • Fixed zombies running outside the map boundaries on the House map.
  • Fixed the next difficulty not unlocking.
  • Fixed the achievement for unlocking the Bestiary.
  • Fixed the flying weapons bug (probably).
  • On PC, fixed the chat scrolling while the player was moving.
  • Attempted to fix small, fast zombies. They sometimes passed through barricades instead of trying to break them.
  • Fixed bugs with the Worker class's barricades.

What's new

  • Increased the radius of the Bear's ability.
  • Increased damage dealt by the Bear's and Sniper's abilities to 200.
  • Added a player list to the pause window in Zombie mode.
  • Added red “RUN TO THE EVACUATION ZONE” text when at least one player is inside the evacuation zone.
  • Increased shotgun damage.

Version 2.0

  • Added new bosses and mini-bosses with unique attacks, effects and minions.
  • Added boss health bars, health scaling based on the number of players and better-balanced waves.
  • Added a Bestiary with unlockable zombie cards and stats.
  • Added an evacuation objective after the 10th zombie wave.
  • Added cloud saves for progress, statistics and Bestiary data.
  • Redesigned the main menu and in-game interfaces.
  • Improved mobile controls and added customizable button and joystick layouts.
  • Added an online player counter and a community button.
  • Weapon vending machines now show the price of weapons and ammunition.
  • Improved lighting quality.
  • Improved connection stability, room joining and returning to the main menu.
  • Improved the initial wave start in single-player mode.
  • Reduced network traffic, CPU load and memory usage.
  • Fixed zombie animations, spawning and movement.
  • Fixed zombie spawning after a host migration.
  • Fixed zombie attacks against players and obstacles.
  • Improved boss attack behavior and effects.
  • Added blood effects and removed unnecessary bullet marks from zombies.
  • Fixed the third-person camera passing through walls.
  • Restored third-person gunshot sounds.
  • Added filtering for chat messages, player names and room names.
  • Added localized ban notifications.
  • Fixed the language display and restored loading-screen localization.
  • Updated the statistics window.
  • Added the Gelios map 🤯 — it is still very unfinished, and you still need to unlock it.
  • Touch controls now let you shoot and move the camera at the same time.
  • Added achievements.
  • Added character classes, each with its own abilities.
  • Zombies now hit players much more reliably instead of attacking empty space.
  • Zombie hits now award slightly fewer coins.
  • Fixed waves ending too early and restored waves correctly after a host migration.
  • Zombies can no longer deal damage through walls or register the same attack multiple times.
  • During the final 10% of a wave, zombies more than 300 meters away now have on-screen and off-screen indicators showing their distance.
  • Switching weapons can no longer be used to skip reloading; quick use of weapons and items still works.
  • Ammo refills now work for previously purchased weapons that are not currently equipped, and money is not deducted when a purchase fails.
  • In PvP, the camera remains controllable during the pre-match countdown while movement and shooting stay locked.
  • Fixed the FPS counter overlapping menu elements.
  • Added missing translations.
  • Added temporary progress recovery after a server disconnect; recovery data is kept for up to 10 minutes.
  • The number of simultaneously active zombies has been increased by two per player.
  • Added armor that can drop from zombies. An option to buy armor will be added as well.
  • High waves can now include up to 150 zombies, depending on the number of players.
  • Every fifth wave — the boss wave — adds five more zombies: 25, 30, 35, 40 and so on.
  • Zombies now spawn at the spawner closest to a player, prioritizing spawners in the most recently unlocked area.
  • Added new sounds, including menu audio, birdsong on the House map and music from Imposter 3D on Gelios.
  • Increased F-Zombie movement speed by 5%.
  • Fixed bonus items spawning behind barricades.
  • Added Normal, Hardcore and Impossible difficulty options, with difficulty-specific achievements.
  • Pausing in offline mode now fully stops the game.
  • Slightly increased shotgun damage.
